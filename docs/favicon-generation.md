## Final generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: browser favicon for Sol Masetti's creative portfolio, square 1024 x 1024 master.
Primary request: Create one elegant, exceptionally legible favicon that belongs to an editorial creative archive brand.
Scene/backdrop: a completely flat solid dark burgundy square, exact color #3b0c21, extending to every edge of the canvas.
Subject: one large uppercase letter S in warm off-white cream, exact color #f3eee8, centered optically. Use a refined editorial serif letterform inspired by classical Didone typography, with broad enough strokes to remain readable at 16 and 32 pixels. The S occupies about 74 percent of the canvas height, with balanced clear space around it.
Style/medium: clean flat graphic mark, crisp edges, minimal, timeless typography.
Text (verbatim): "S"
Constraints: Only one S. Square front-facing flat artwork, no perspective, no texture, no gradients, no shadow, no framing, no mockup, no extra text, no watermark, no tiny decorative elements. Strong immediately recognizable silhouette at favicon scale.
